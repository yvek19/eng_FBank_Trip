    Aurora & Cold Tips

    Tips

    AURORA TRACKER

    Can we see the aurora right now?

    Explore Fairbanks runs a live aurora tracker that shows the current chance of seeing the lights, based on aurora activity, weather and daylight.

    Open the live aurora tracker ↗

    Seeing the aurora

    • Best hours are usually 22:00 – 02:00. Clear skies matter most.
    • Check the aurora forecast and cloud cover before heading out.
    UAF aurora forecast ↗

    Photographing it

    • Try ISO 1600–3200, a 5–15 second shutter and the widest aperture.
    • On a phone, use night mode with the phone on a tripod.

    Extreme cold

    • Dress in layers: a wicking base, a warm middle layer and a windproof shell.
    • Cold drains phones and power banks fast. Keep them in an inside pocket.

    Short days

    • Around the solstice there are only about 3–4 hours of daylight: light around 11 AM, dark by about 3 PM.
    • Plan outdoor activities around midday.
